# CIRIS reality compiler

The **CIRIS reality compiler** is the [holon](holon.md) applied to a model of physical reality. A compiler lowers a program through a stack of representations, and each step is checked by a commuting square. The reality compiler does the same for a physical model, tier by tier:

- Each tier is certified **relative to the tier below**: against the fine model's dynamics, not against nature.
- A tier is admitted when it preserves **what the level above reads**, within a stated budget, and it pays a stated price for what it drops. This is weaker than exact preservation, which is what a compiler square gives.
- A generated scene, which has no fine motion, is certified differently, by a section law and a transport cost.
- Where no certificate exists, the compiler **refuses by name**. Examples are the quantum refusals past the coherence wall and a sector dimension above its cap. It does not emit an approximation it cannot certify.

The result is one engine for physics and graphics, measured so far from a browser (WASM) to a multi-core machine and one GPU. Results are deterministic and auditable at every tier it runs. This application is foundational to CIRIS.

As an analogy only, it treats a simulated world the way the [CIRIS Kernel](kernel.md) treats a governed group: everyone holding the same inputs reaches the same answer, with no authority in the middle. The Kernel's checkpoint and the holon's Closed are the same lemma in two places. That counts as one result, not two independent confirmations.

## What it does not claim

It makes no claim about nature directly. Each tier is certified against a derived model ("Not licensed: … anything about real water (the carrier is a derived model)", CIRISHolon `TIERS.md` §5).

## Scope of priority claim

- Compiler correctness as a commuting diagram is classical (McCarthy and Painter, 1967; Morris, 1973).
- The square for abstractions of dynamics is also standard: bisimulation, abstract interpretation and lumpability. See [holon](holon.md).
- The phrase "reality compiler" may have scattered informal prior use, and *RealityEngine* (Silicon Graphics, 1992) names an unrelated system.

CIRIS claims nothing on the bare phrase or on the square. It claims priority on the **application**, the holon as a certified, tier-by-tier compilation of a physical model with refusal by name (from 2026-08-26), and on the compound **"CIRIS reality compiler"** from this record.
