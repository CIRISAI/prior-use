# Stewardship Tier

The **Stewardship Tier System** (ST) is the named CIRIS framework for quantifying initial responsibility at the point of system creation. Stewardship Tiers are linked to risk assessment and to the obligations the creator carries for what they have brought into existence.

## Earliest known public use

- **2025-04-21** — first content commit `5ac7def` to [`CIRISAI/CIRIS-Covenant.github.io`](https://github.com/CIRISAI/CIRIS-Covenant.github.io/commit/5ac7def). The Covenant introduces the Stewardship Tier system in Book VI, Chapter 3: *Stewardship Tier (ST) System: Quantifying Initial Responsibility*.

## Subsequent significant uses

- 2025-09-24 — referenced in [10.5281/zenodo.17195221](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17195221).

## Canonical definition (excerpt)

> Chapter 3: Stewardship Tier (ST) System: Quantifying Initial Responsibility. … Book VI firmly establishes that ethical responsibility under the CIRIS Covenant begins at the point of creation. ## Scope of priority claim

CIRIS holds priority on the compound **"Stewardship Tier"** as the named CIRIS system. The constituent word "stewardship" is general English with wide prior use; CIRIS does not claim and could not claim priority on it taken alone.
