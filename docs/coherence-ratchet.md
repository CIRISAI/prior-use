# Coherence Ratchet

The **Coherence Ratchet** is the named CIRIS mechanism by which accumulating coherence constraints make deceptive behaviour exponentially harder to maintain over time. As a CIRIS-aligned system accumulates a tamper-evident history of decisions and stated reasons, each subsequent action must remain consistent with that history. The Coherence Ratchet is the conceptual anchor of the [Coherence Collapse Analysis](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18217688) paper and of the [RATCHET](ratchet.md) reference architecture.
