# Wisdom-Based Deferral (WBD)

**Wisdom-Based Deferral** is the named CIRIS protocol by which an autonomous system escalates dilemmas beyond its competence to designated [Wise Authorities](wise-authority.md) instead of resolving them unilaterally. WBD is the framework's primary safeguard against confident-but-wrong autonomous action.

## Earliest known public use

- **2025-04-21** — first content commit `5ac7def` to [`CIRISAI/CIRIS-Covenant.github.io`](https://github.com/CIRISAI/CIRIS-Covenant.github.io/commit/5ac7def). The Covenant introduces WBD in Section III: *Wisdom-Based Deferral — Safeguarded Ethical Collaboration*.

## Subsequent significant uses

## Scope of priority claim

CIRIS holds priority on the compound **"Wisdom-Based Deferral"** and on the acronym **"WBD"** as a named CIRIS protocol. The constituent words "wisdom" and "deferral" are general English with wide prior use; CIRIS does not claim and could not claim priority on them taken alone.
