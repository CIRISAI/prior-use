# CIRISClient: the screen

**CIRISClient** is the screen of the CIRIS mesh: the shipping Kotlin Multiplatform client for Android, iOS, desktop and browser (WASM). It began inside CIRISAgent as the agent's mobile app and was spun out as its own repository.

Its idea of what a screen is inspired [CEWPOS](cewpos.md)'s view-forms and Scene IR. Its surfaces are specified by [CIRIS Specification Documents](csd.md). The same line leads to [holon-draw](holon-draw.md), where a drawn scene is a view of a list.

## Earliest known public use

- **2025-12-23**: [`CIRISAgent@4c25b5af7`](https://github.com/CIRISAI/CIRISAgent/commit/4c25b5af7), *"feat: Add Kotlin Multiplatform mobile scaffold for unified Android/iOS apps"*.

## Subsequent significant uses

- 2026-04-20: CIRISAgent release 2.6.0 adds WASM infrastructure and verification of the audit chain in the client.
- 2026-06-27: [CEWPOS](cewpos.md) generalises the screen into zero-authority view-forms.
- 2026-08-16: [`CIRISAI/CIRISClient`](https://github.com/CIRISAI/CIRISClient) created ([`a218a8c6`](https://github.com/CIRISAI/CIRISClient/commit/a218a8c6)). The client is vendored from CIRISAgent on 2026-08-20.
- 2026-09-07 onward: surfaces are governed by [CSDs](csd.md).

## Scope of priority claim

Kotlin Multiplatform and Compose are JetBrains and Google technologies, and a cross-platform client is common practice. CIRIS claims priority on the name **"CIRISClient"** and on its specific lineage: a client whose surfaces are specified and tested by the same documents.
