# CIRISOntology

**CIRISOntology** is the CIRIS research programme that states what we think is true, how we decide what is true (epistemology), and how we decide what matters (axiomology). The claims are machine-checked in Lean, and each one carries the observation that would prove it wrong. The holon object that [CIRISHolon](holon.md) is built on was developed and checked here first.

## Earliest known public use

- **2026-07-22**: [`CIRISOntology@f8f9011`](https://github.com/CIRISAI/CIRISOntology/commit/f8f9011), *"Seed CIRISOntology: the stance, the epistemology, the axiomology"*. Repository created the same day.

## Subsequent significant uses

- 2026-08-26: the theorems are carried verbatim into [CIRISHolon](holon.md) as the engine's contract.

## Scope of priority claim

CIRIS claims priority on the name **"CIRISOntology"** and on the specific stance, theorems and falsifiers it publishes. "Ontology", "epistemology" and "axiology" are long-established terms in philosophy and computer science. CIRIS claims nothing on them.
