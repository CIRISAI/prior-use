# Alignment Manifold

The **Alignment Manifold** is the named geometric construction in the CIRIS framework that represents the space of CIRIS-aligned reasoning shapes. The [Coherence](coherence-ratchet.md) faculty's structural detection computes distance from this manifold; the [Coherence Collapse Analysis](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18217688) paper provides the mathematical machinery that the Alignment Manifold concept depends on (constraint-manifold superposition under the J-function).
