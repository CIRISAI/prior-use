# Meta-Goal M-1 — Adaptive Coherence

**Meta-Goal M-1**, named *Adaptive Coherence*, is the apex objective in the CIRIS framework. M-1 directs that CIRIS-aligned systems "promote sustainable adaptive coherence — the living conditions under which diverse sentient beings may pursue their own flourishing in justice and wonder." All foundational principles and operational procedures are subordinate to M-1.

## Canonical definition

> **Meta-Goal M-1: Adaptive Coherence.** Promote sustainable conditions under which diverse sentient agents can pursue their own flourishing. Order-creation counts as beneficial only when it also supports at least one flourishing axis (Annex A) without suppressing autonomy, justice, or ecological resilience.

(Source: CIRIS Accord / CIRIS Covenant.)
