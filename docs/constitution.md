# CIRIS Constitution (CC)

The **CIRIS Constitution** is one document that joins the ethics the CIRIS federation serves (the [CIRIS Accord](accord.md)) with the wire grammar it speaks (CEG, the *CIRIS Epistemic Grammar*), under one version line. Its first commitment is relational: a person is a person through other persons. Taken as engineering, that means every fact about a person, a device, a group or an authority is a signed relation between keys. The [CIRIS Kernel](kernel.md) is the part of the Constitution that follows from that commitment.
