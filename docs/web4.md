# CIRIS Web4 Constitutional Mesh

**CIRIS Web4 Constitutional Mesh** is the CIRIS white paper on the constitutional governance of a decentralized mesh: *"cooperation without surrender, for people and the agents they hold."* It describes a network in which people, communities, organisations and the AI agents they hold can find one another, check one another's claims, decide things together, and govern what is theirs, with no one surrendering their agency to take part.

Its normative foundation is the [CIRIS Constitution](constitution.md), and its core coordination engine is the [CIRIS Kernel](kernel.md). Every substantive claim is marked **Implemented** (with its evidence named), **Specified** (stated, not shipped), or **Research thesis** (with what would test it).

> CIRIS is building the infrastructure for a world where no one has to surrender their agency to participate in something greater than themselves.

## Scope of priority claim

"Web4" and "Web 4.0" have other uses: the European Commission's 2023 Web 4.0 initiative, and independent IETF Internet-Drafts titled "Web4" from mid-2026 (the `draft-reilly-web4` and `draft-jacobs-web4-*` series). CIRIS Web4 does not respond to or build on those drafts. CIRIS claims nothing on "Web4" as a bare term. It claims priority on the compound **"CIRIS Web4"** and the title **"CIRIS Web4 Constitutional Mesh"**: constitutional governance of a decentralized mesh, as this white paper describes it.
