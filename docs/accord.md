# CIRIS Accord

The **CIRIS Accord** is the current name of the CIRIS framework's normative document. The document was originally titled **[CIRIS Covenant](covenant.md)** from 2025-04-21 and was renamed to *CIRIS Accord* on 2026-02-18. Document content lineage is continuous through the rename — the same principles, books, annexes, [PDMA](pdma.md), [WBD](wbd.md), [Wise Authority](wise-authority.md), [Stewardship Tier system](stewardship-tier.md), and [Meta-Goal M-1](m1-adaptive-coherence.md) carry forward unchanged in substance.

The current published version is *CIRIS Accord Version 1.2-Beta — Working Draft (Open to Adversarial Review)*.

## Earliest known public use

The Accord's priority is recorded along two axes:

- **Document-content lineage**: 2025-04-21 — content first published as the [CIRIS Covenant](covenant.md) at [`CIRIS-Covenant.github.io@5ac7def`](https://github.com/CIRISAI/CIRIS-Covenant.github.io/commit/5ac7def). The document text states an issuance date of 2025-04-16.
- **Named compound *CIRIS Accord***: 2026-02-18 — name adopted on this date in coordinated commits across [`ciris-website@b925457`](https://github.com/CIRISAI/ciris-website/commit/b925457) (*"refactor: Rename covenant to accord across codebase"*) and [`CIRISAgent@daad5f08c`](https://github.com/CIRISAI/CIRISAgent/commit/daad5f08c) (*"Rename covenant→accord across codebase"*).

## Scope of priority claim

CIRIS holds priority on:

- The **document content** — continuously from 2025-04-21 via the [Covenant](covenant.md) lineage.
