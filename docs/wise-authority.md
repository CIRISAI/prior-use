# Wise Authority (WA)

A **Wise Authority** is the named human (or human-led group) to which a CIRIS-aligned system escalates a decision via [Wisdom-Based Deferral](wbd.md) when the dilemma exceeds the system's competence. WAs are designated in advance, accountable, and operate under documented governance.

## Scope of priority claim

CIRIS holds priority on the compound **"Wise Authority"** and the acronym **"WA"** as a named CIRIS role. The constituent words "wise" and "authority" are general English with wide prior use; CIRIS does not claim and could not claim priority on them taken alone.
