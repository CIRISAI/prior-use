# CIRIS Covenant

The **CIRIS Covenant** was the original name of the CIRIS framework's normative document, in force from 2025-04-21 through 2026-02-18. The document was renamed to **[CIRIS Accord](accord.md)** on 2026-02-18 across all CIRISAI repositories. Document content lineage is continuous through the rename — the same text, principles, books, and annexes carry forward; the name is what changed.

The Covenant set out the foundational principles, [PDMA](pdma.md), [Wisdom-Based Deferral](wbd.md), [Wise Authority](wise-authority.md), [Stewardship Tier system](stewardship-tier.md), and [Meta-Goal M-1 (Adaptive Coherence)](m1-adaptive-coherence.md) that constitute the operational core of the framework.

## Earliest known public use

> CIRIS Covenant Version 1.0-β — Risk-Limited Release. … This specification governs the day-to-day ethical operation of advanced autonomous systems. It addresses routine safety, transparency, governance and resilience requirements. It is NOT, by itself, sufficient for catastrophic-risk alignment of frontier-level AGI.

(Source: `public/ciris_covenant.txt`.)

## Scope of priority claim

CIRIS holds priority on the compound **"CIRIS Covenant"** as the historical title of the named document for the period 2025-04-21 through 2026-02-18, and on the *document content* it canonicalized — which carries forward without break into [CIRIS Accord](accord.md). The constituent word "covenant" is general English with very wide prior use; CIRIS does not claim and could not claim priority on the bare word.
