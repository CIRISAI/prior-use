# PDMA — Principled Decision-Making Algorithm

The **PDMA** (Principled Decision-Making Algorithm) is the named CIRIS procedure that translates the framework's foundational principles and meta-goal [M-1](m1-adaptive-coherence.md) into action-level decisions. The PDMA evaluates each candidate action against all core principles and against M-1, and records its rationale in a tamper-evident decision trace.

## Earliest known public use

- **2025-04-21** — first content commit `5ac7def` to [`CIRISAI/CIRIS-Covenant.github.io`](https://github.com/CIRISAI/CIRIS-Covenant.github.io/commit/5ac7def). The Covenant introduces the PDMA in Section II.

## Scope of priority claim

CIRIS holds priority on the compound **"Principled Decision-Making Algorithm"** and on the acronym **"PDMA"** as a named CIRIS procedure. The general phrase "principled decision-making" is ordinary English; CIRIS does not claim and could not claim priority on it.
